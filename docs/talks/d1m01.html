演讲骨架

  1. AI Engineer 是以动手构建真实产品和可靠基础设施的工程师为中心的社区，而不是谈论 AI 抢工作或请政要发言的会议。
  2. 上海站是 AIE 在全球的第六个合作站点，也是第一次进入中国，后续还将去印度和非洲，目标是推动这类工程师对话。
  3. 请参会者自己拍照、录像、发推传播，因为这是第一场没有直播的 AIE。

信号

  • 市场格局 / 规律AIE 品牌通过各地合作伙伴向全球扩张，并开始进入中国；参会的既有基础设施、应用公司，也有模型实验室，社区正在成为 AI 工程生态的连接点。

It's so exciting to have the first Chinese AIE. We've been really trying to build this community of technical engineers, people who are building real things with AI, not getting caught up in the hype or safety fears or anything, just trying to really build things that people use, build infrastructure that is reliable, build the future. That's why it's focused on engineers, right? I think a lot of these kinds of AI conferences usually feature politicians, heads of state or heads of companies that just talk about, you know, will AI take my job and all those things that are very memorable. This is the sixth AIE partner that we've had around the world. We have had Singapore, Miami, Sydney, Paris, and Melbourne, and we're going to have India and Africa next year as well. But entering China is such an important step for us because we want to promote this kind of discussion. And I hope you are here because you want to do that too. seg 43–48

I find that when people come to an AIE, they have a different kind of conversation. Even the speakers are so excited because they get to have a relationship with attendees that they never have at any other conference. I hope you have that as well. I'm really jealous. I wish I could come. We are deep in the process of AIE Code. You can see all the boxes here, where we're getting and assembling all of that. Hopefully next year I'll be able to join you. seg 49–56

Everyone wants to see you succeed. There's a lot of interest in the model side and the infrastructure side, but even on the apps that you are representing. And also all the labs, which we really care a lot about, like StepFun, Microsoft, World Labs, and HeyGen. Bin, if you're there, Bin is actually flying back to help us with AIE Code as well. I don't know how he's doing so much travel, but he's doing great. Ryo as well has spoken with us in Singapore. So many friends there. I'm so jealous. I wish I could come, but I also have to try to focus on myself. But I wish you all the best. Have a great level of conversation. seg 57–73

One thing you can do for us is to tweet and share all the videos and photos. We cannot live stream this conference. It's the first AIE that hasn't been live streamed ever. But we can live through you, by your notes and your photos, your videos, whatever you want to share. I'm really looking forward to that, and I'm going to be sitting here just retweeting, reading and cataloging everything. I'm so excited. Thank you so much. seg 74–79
